# Charm questions for Angel

Every other charm is done (`info/charm-implementation.md`). These are the ones I wasn't
sure about. Each has the relevant charm text, what is already built, and my suggestion.
Answer with the number and "yes", "no" or your own call.

## 1. Flow Like Blood (Universal, p. 185)

> Commit 1 mote for the scene. The Exalt adds 1 to her Defense against attacks at close
> range and steals 1 Power from her attacker after successfully defending.

**Built:** 1 commit dot + in-use icon. **Open:** the +1 Defense only counts against
attacks from close range. **Suggestion:** add +1 to Parry and Evasion while in use (most
attacks you defend in melee are at close range), with its own line in the hint. Or leave
it off the sheet.

## 2. Four Magical Materials Form (Martial Arts, p. 296)

> Commit 2 motes … The Exalt chooses one of the following benefits, which they may change
> reflexively on Step 1 … Orichalcum: Hardness increases by 2 and Soak increases by 3 …

**Built:** 2 commit dots + in-use icon. **Open:** only Orichalcum changes the sheet.
**Suggestion:** a Jade / Moonsilver / Orichalcum / Starmetal switch in the charm window;
with Orichalcum picked and the charm in use, Soak +3 and Hardness +2.

## 3. Snake Form, Water Dragon Form, Hungry All-Consuming Cloud

> Snake Form: "Set the Exalt's base Soak equal to half their Finesse, rounded down."
> Water Dragon Form: "may set Soak equal to half her Force, rounded down."
> Hungry All-Consuming Cloud: "gains her Fortitude in temporary Bruised Health levels."

**Built:** their commit dots + in-use icons. **Open:** the sheet has the nine classic
Attributes, not Force/Finesse/Fortitude. **Suggestion:** read Finesse as Dexterity, Force
as Strength and Fortitude as Stamina, and apply them while in use (Snake/Water: only when
it raises Soak).

## 4. Hybrid Body Transformation — Deadly Beast Transformation (Lunar, p. 259)

> Repurchase: The character may enter a frenzied, hybrid war form … increases her Hardness
> and base Soak by 1 each … While transformed in this way, decrease her Defense by 1.

**Built:** 2 commit dots + in-use icon, limit 2. **Open:** the war form is a choice each
time. **Suggestion:** a "War form" switch in the charm window (once repurchased): Hardness
+1, Soak +1, Parry and Evasion −1 while on.

## 5. Life-Scaffolding Survival (Liminal, p. 258)

> Reduce all wound penalties by one and ignore the effects of any Dramatic Injuries.
> Increase Soak and Hardness by the Liminal's wound penalties and increase Defense by 1 if
> the Liminal has any Dramatic Injuries.

**Built:** 1 commit dot + in-use icon. **Open:** is the Soak/Hardness bonus the penalty
before or after the −1? And the sheet doesn't track Dramatic Injuries. **Suggestion:**
wound penalty −1, then Soak and Hardness + the penalty *after* the −1; the Defense +1 left
to the manual box.

## 6. Undying Body's aspect modes (Liminal, p. 254)

> All modes cost one Health level to activate, unless the Liminal is in the respective
> Nature. Blood: increasing Defense by one … Flesh: gaining one additional Soak …

**Built:** Soak +2, Hardness +2, wound penalty −1 and the Essence 3/5 Bruised levels.
**Open:** the aspect mode is optional (it costs a Health level). **Suggestion:** a
"with aspect mode" switch in the charm window; on, a Blood Liminal gets Parry and Evasion
+1 and a Flesh Liminal Soak +1 more.

## 7. Beast-Uplifting Harmony (Universal, p. 200)

> The Exalt may choose up to her Essence of the following benefits for her familiar; she
> automatically gains additional benefits as her Essence increases.

**Built (before):** each purchase binds a familiar and picks its benefits. **Open:** there
is no way to add benefits later when Essence rises. **Suggestion:** an "Add benefit"
button per familiar in the charm window, up to Essence. Or leave it (it's the familiar's
sheet, not yours).

## 8. Quickening the Forge — Solar mode (Universal, p. 195)

> Supreme Perfection of Style: Select a type of craft … You gain an additional field of
> specialty at Essence 3 and Essence 5.

**Built:** 2 commit dots + in-use icon. **Open:** only Solars choose a craft, and more at
Essence 3 and 5. **Suggestion:** leave it to the Notes (no sheet effect), or ask Solars for
the craft when bought.

## 9. Wyld-Shaping Technique (Solar) and Constructive Convergence of Principles (Infernal)

> Wyld Cauldron Technology: Maintaining the reality of Wyld-created Merits requires a
> commitment of 1 mote per rating (1 for tertiary, 2 for secondary, 3 for primary).

**Open:** how many commit dots? It depends on how many Merits you keep. **Suggestion:** 3
dots once the upgrade is unlocked (one primary Merit); you'd use the Committed counter for
more.

## 10. Investiture of Flesh (Liminal, p. 257)

> Spend up the Liminal's Essence in motes … Alternatively, the Liminal may commit those
> motes for the scene in order to allow a friendly ghost to materialize.

**Open:** dots per Essence. **Suggestion:** 5 dots plus an in-use icon (light as many as
you commit; your Essence is the real cap).

## 11. Manifold Hunter's Tactics (Lunar, p. 259)

> The Lunar gains one other method of learning a shape listed under Lunar Advantages
> (p. 52) … Repurchase: Gain access to two of the below methods [seven listed].

**Built:** limit 2. **Open:** the first purchase picks from the Lunar Advantages list
(not in the app), the repurchase picks two of the seven listed here. **Suggestion:** first
purchase asks for the method by name (typed), the repurchase picks two from the seven.

## 12. Naked Soul Insight is in the library twice

One copy is the real charm (Awareness, p. 244); the other is a stub whose text is only
"See p. 244" (ability Embassy). Nobody has bought either. **Suggestion:** delete the stub
in Admin → Charms (I didn't, since deleting library data is your call).
